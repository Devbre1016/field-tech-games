Tips

  • Off a ramp the nose tends to drop: hold gas in the air to pull it back up, tap brake to tip it down. Land on both wheels.
  • Normal landings are fine; nose-dives and flat drops from big air crack cylinders.
  • Machines on site: a swinging crane load, and loaders / dump trucks that cross the road (flashing beacon + beeps first). Wait for your gap; a hit knocks you back and costs integrity.
  • Plywood barricades, bucket stacks and crates smash apart: just drive through.
  • Gear unlocks as you go: Low range after job 2, the Nitro Kit after job 3, the Winch after the Home County boss. Buy it in the Garage: later jobs list what they require.
  • Sheer clay walls, open excavations and washouts are too steep to drive: stop at the bottom and winch (X) to the ⚓ flag on top.
  • Short on cash for the next job's gear? Replay finished jobs in rain, snow, heat or at night for a pay bonus, or at sunrise / sunset.
  • Mud gets stickier the slower you go: carry speed in, and back out (←) for another run.
  • Missed a boost jump? Drive or crawl (C) up the far slope, or winch (X) out of the gully. Nitro gaps (🔥 signs) need nitro.
  • Phone: ◀ BRAKE and GAS ▶ (they also tilt the truck in the air), plus ⛰ LOW, ⚓ WINCH and 🔥 NITRO.
